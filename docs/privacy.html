Privacy policy

Last updated October 22, 2026

The short version

Asocial collects nothing. It has no server, no analytics, no advertising, and no accounts.

What stays on your device

  • Your sign-ins to Instagram, YouTube, and Facebook, kept by the iOS system web view the same way Safari keeps them. Settings › Sign Out of Everything removes them.
  • Your filter settings and the count of distractions skipped today.

The sites you use

When you use Instagram, YouTube, or Facebook in Asocial, you talk to those sites directly, and their own privacy policies apply. Asocial doesn't see, store, or forward what you do there.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/djui/asocial.
